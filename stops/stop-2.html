Trail Stop 2: Native Papyrus & Natural Water Filtration

Listen to how native wetland plants clean Kigali's water:

Stop 2 Narration

Nature's Water Treatment Plant

  • Dense papyrus reeds slow down fast rainwater runoff from surrounding hills, trapping soil and sediment.
  • Microorganisms living around plant roots absorb excess nutrients and pollutants before water flows downstream.

Responsible Visitor Tip

Do not pick papyrus stems or throw anything into the water channels—even small items disrupt water flow.
