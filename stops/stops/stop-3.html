Wetland Wildlife & Birdlife

  • Wetlands provide food, shelter, and nesting cover for herons, egrets, kingfishers, warblers, and seasonal migrants.
  • Healthy bird populations are a natural sign that the wetland ecosystem is balanced and thriving.

Responsible Visitor Tip

Use headphones when listening to audio guides, move calmly, and never chase or feed wild birds.
